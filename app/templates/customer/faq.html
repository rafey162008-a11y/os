Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse products, add them to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can pay via cash on delivery or card.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash on Delivery, Credit/Debit Cards, and Bank Transfer.

How can I track my order?

Use the "Track Order" feature and enter your order number, or view it in your account under "My Orders".

What is your return policy?

You can request a return within 7 days of delivery for eligible products. Go to "My Orders" and click "Return".

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.

Visit our Help Center or contact us.
